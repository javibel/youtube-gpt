## Full description

YTubViral is an all-in-one AI platform for YouTube creators who want to grow their channel with data and AI — not guesswork.

It includes 14 tools:

- SEO Score — Analyze any video's SEO with a 0-100 score and actionable checklist
- Keyword Research — Competition level, opportunity score, and related terms for any search query
- Competitor Analysis — Subscribers, upload frequency, average views, and top keywords from any channel
- AI Title Generator — 5 viral title options optimized for CTR, powered by Claude AI
- Full Video Scripts — Complete scripts with intro, sections, hooks, and outro
- SEO Descriptions — Optimized descriptions with timestamps and keywords
- Captions & Hashtags — Ready-to-post social media content
- A/B Testing — Compare two titles or thumbnails head-to-head with real data
- Trending Explorer — Discover trending videos by country, category, language, and duration
- Revenue Estimator — Estimate CPM and monthly revenue for any channel (40+ countries)
- Retention Analyzer — Identify drop-off points and get AI pacing advice
- Subscriber Analysis — Demographics breakdown and AI audience profile
- AI Content Calendar — Smart scheduling based on trends and competitor patterns
- AI Coach — Chat assistant that knows your channel and gives personalized advice in 4 modes (create, analyze, optimize, research)

Plus a Chrome Extension that shows SEO scores, keyword stats, outlier detection, and competitor data directly inside YouTube and YouTube Studio.

Free plan includes unlimited keyword research, competitor analysis, and SEO scoring. Pro plan (9.99 EUR/month) unlocks all 14 tools with 200 AI generations. Business plan (29.99 EUR/month) adds unlimited AI and team collaboration.

Built by a solo developer. Fully bilingual (English + Spanish).
