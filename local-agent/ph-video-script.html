Recording Tips

  1. Clean the browser: Close all other tabs, hide bookmarks bar, disable extensions with badges
  2. Use a clean profile or incognito: No personal data visible
  3. Mouse movements: Smooth and deliberate, not rushed. Use a mouse, not trackpad
  4. Wait for loads: Don't click before the page has fully loaded
  5. Screen resolution: 1920x1080 or 2560x1440 (scales down well)
  6. Music: Use royalty-free music — "Chill Electronic" from YouTube Audio Library, or lo-fi beats from Pixabay. Keep it low volume.
  7. Text overlays: Add in post. White text, semi-transparent dark background, bottom-left or top-center.
  8. Transitions: Simple crossfade between scenes (0.3s). No fancy effects.
  9. Total time target: 70–80 seconds ideal. Under 90 max.
