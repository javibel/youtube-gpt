## Recording Tips

1. **Clean the browser:** Close all other tabs, hide bookmarks bar, disable extensions that show badges
2. **Use a clean profile or incognito:** No personal data visible
3. **Mouse movements:** Smooth and deliberate, not rushed. Use a mouse, not trackpad
4. **Wait for loads:** Don't click before the page has fully loaded
5. **Screen resolution:** 1920x1080 or 2560x1440 (will scale down well)
6. **Music:** Use royalty-free music. Suggestions:
   - "Chill Electronic" from YouTube Audio Library
   - Any lo-fi beat from Pixabay
   - Keep it low volume, it's ambient
7. **Text overlays:** Add in post (CapCut, DaVinci Resolve, or even Canva Video). Use white text, semi-transparent dark background, bottom-left or top-center
8. **Transitions:** Simple crossfade between scenes (0.3s). No fancy effects
9. **Total time target:** 70-80 seconds ideal. Under 90 max
