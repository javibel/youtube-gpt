# Revenue analysis boundary

An explicit POST captures language, USD/EUR currency and optional manual net RPM
before the native cookie/account/paid-plan/channel/token checks. The original 55s
HTTP lifecycle bounds body/source/model/response work. Expired tokens are rejected;
there is no OAuth refresh, token write, retry or recovery from metadata. GET cannot
start analysis. Every dependent source/model action and final response recheck the
captured native context. The client accepts only its own actor/session/command,
form assumptions, currency and requested range; edits or retired views suppress
late results. A serialized/copy receipt cannot originate or recover a reader.

The requested interval has 28 inclusive dates ending two days before the native
request date. Independent channel-total, top-20 country, top-15 video and daily
reports do not establish identical coverage, complete geography, or freshness.
Missing rows remain absent. Metadata is checked against the original own channel;
no API-key/public-search fallback or inferred title is used.

YouTube `estimatedRevenue` remains an adjustable estimate, in the explicitly
requested currency. Empty or unavailable monetary reports are not zero earnings or
proof of nonmonetization. The manual net RPM scenario uses reported channel views
and the user's explicit assumption. It never replaces the reported estimate. No
country CPM default, view-share revenue allocation or monthly/yearly extrapolation
is produced. Model suggestions are qualitative hypotheses with explicit limits;
invalid/unavailable model output leaves metrics without tips and never retries.

See [metrics](https://developers.google.com/youtube/analytics/metrics),
[channel reports](https://developers.google.com/youtube/analytics/channel_reports)
and [query currency/coverage](https://developers.google.com/youtube/analytics/reference/reports/query).
Local tests use closed source/model/task-store fixtures and fresh SQL accounts.
They establish implementation/local regression, not real OAuth consent, source
provenance, freshness, monetary/model quality or operational acceptance.
